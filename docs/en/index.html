Why Daling

How many windows did you open today?

Course files and assignments live on the LMS, events and admin notices arrive by webmail, exam and registration dates sit in the academic calendar, and scholarship and job news is on the school notice board. Separate logins, separate checks, and it's easy to miss a deadline.

Daling brings it all into one screen

Features

Leave it running. It collects for you.

Sign in once and Daling checks the LMS and your school mail on a schedule, fetching only what's new.

Dashboard · Deadlines

Today's to-dos and deadlines come first

The first screen shows your remaining classes, assignments due within 7 days and unread mail in one line. Every assignment from every course is listed by D-day.

  • "Go submit" opens the LMS submission page for any unsubmitted assignment
  • D-day for exams and registration, plus a weekly timetable
  • Import your timetable from an Everytime link or a screenshot

Course files

The LMS folder structure, kept up to date

New lecture files are found and downloaded automatically, organized exactly like the week and lecture folders on the LMS.

  • Uploaded to your Google Drive by course and week
  • Also copied to a folder on your computer or to OneDrive, iCloud Drive and more
  • Open, save or upload many files at once (on Windows, send to Samsung Notes too)

School mail

Read and write school mail inside the app

No separate webmail login: read, reply and attach right in Daling. Mail is sorted into categories automatically.

  • Seminars & events, admin & student teams, careers and more
  • Recipient autocomplete from the directory and past mail, attachments, drafts
  • Translate or summarize mail (optional, with your own Gemini key)

Calendar

Even event dates inside e-mails land on your calendar

Assignment deadlines, the academic calendar, holidays and your own plans in one month view. Daling also reads event and seminar dates from your mail.

  • Deadlines in red, school events in yellow, your plans in blue
  • Synced automatically to a dedicated Google Calendar
  • Export deadlines as a calendar file (.ics)

Get started

From install to first sync in minutes

Download & install

On Windows, run the installer. On a Mac, open the DMG and drag Daling into Applications.

Add your school account

Enter your LMS and school mail sign-in in Settings. Passwords are encrypted and stay on this computer.

Connect Google (optional)

Only if you want Drive and Calendar. Daling works fine without it.

That's it

Dalgu gives you a tour on first launch. After that, just leave it running.

Privacy

Runs only on your computer

There is no developer-operated server. Your files and account details are stored only on your computer and are never sent to the developer.

  • Passwords and API keys encrypted with Windows DPAPI or the macOS Keychain
  • No analytics, ads or usage tracking; nothing is ever sold
  • Connecting Google is optional and can be revoked any time

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no payments, and the source code is public.

Is this an official DGIST app?

No. It's an unofficial app made by a DGIST student and is not made or operated by DGIST. Always double-check important deadlines and notices on the LMS and official channels.

Can the developer see my password?

No. Passwords are encrypted on your computer (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain) and never sent anywhere. The app signs in to the school servers (LMS, mail) directly.

Why does it ask for Google permissions?

To upload course files to your Drive (drive.file, limited to files the app creates) and add deadlines and events to your Calendar. It's optional, and you can revoke access any time under Google Account → Third-party connections.

macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened".

That's the quarantine flag macOS adds to downloads. Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Daling.app in Terminal and open it again.

Does uninstalling delete my files?

No. Files and settings stay in the data folder (Windows C:\lms-autosaver, Mac ~/.lms-autosaver). Delete that folder if you no longer need it.

Where do I send feedback or bug reports?

Use the "Feedback" button at the bottom left of the app, or write to tutleblue12@gmail.com.
