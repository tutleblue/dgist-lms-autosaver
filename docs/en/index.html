Why Daling

How many windows did you open today?

The LMS, webmail, the academic calendar and the notice board. Separate logins, separate checks, and it's easy to miss a deadline.

Daling brings it all into one screen

Features

Leave it running. It collects for you.

Sign in once and Daling checks mail every 5 minutes, deadlines every hour and course files every 3 hours, fetching only what's new.

Dashboard · Deadlines

Today's to-dos and deadlines come first

The first screen shows your remaining classes, assignments due within 7 days and unread mail.

  • Every assignment from every course in one list, sorted by D-day
  • "Go submit" opens the LMS submission page for an unsubmitted assignment
  • D-day for exams and registration, plus a weekly timetable

Course files

The LMS folder structure, kept up to date

Only new lecture files are downloaded, organized exactly like the week and lecture folders on the LMS.

  • Also sorted by course in a folder on your computer
  • Pick many files and save them at once (on Windows, send to Samsung Notes too)

School mail

Read and write school mail inside the app

No separate webmail login: read your mail and reply right in Daling.

  • Sorted automatically: seminars & events, admin & student teams, careers and more
  • Translate or summarize mail (optional, turn on in Settings)

Calendar

Deadlines, school dates and your plans in one calendar

Assignment deadlines, the academic calendar and holidays fill in on their own, and you can add your own plans.

  • Deadlines in red, school events in yellow, your plans in blue
  • Export deadlines as a calendar file (.ics)

Tips

Small features that save a lot of time

Things you couldn't do with the LMS and webmail on their own.

Courses · Mail

Professors, TAs and their mail, by course name

  • Type a course name such as General Physics in the To field and its professor appears with the section, along with any TAs you've exchanged mail with.
  • Each course card lists this term's professors and TAs with their section. Click the envelope to start a mail with the subject [Course, Section 01] filled in.
  • Search "General Chemistry II", "General Chemistry 2" or the Korean course name to get that course's mail. Senders get a tag such as "General Physics II · Sec. 3 · Professor".

Privacy

Runs only on your own computer

There is no developer-operated server. Your files and account details are stored on your own computer and are never sent to the developer. Only the features you turn on (Google Drive and Calendar, the AI assistant) send data, directly to that service.

  • Passwords and API keys encrypted on your own computer (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, Linux keyring)
  • No analytics, ads or usage tracking; nothing is ever sold
  • Connecting Google is optional; revoke it any time in your Google Account

Read the full Privacy Policy →

Google Drivedrive.file

Uploads course files into course/week folders the app creates and checks for duplicates. The app cannot see Drive files it did not create.

Google Calendarcalendar.app.created

Creates one dedicated calendar and adds or updates deadlines, academic events and dates found in mail there. It can only open calendars the app created, so your other calendars and events stay out of reach.

Other connections

DGIST LMS and mail (with your school account), the school website and public data (public info only), GitHub for update checks. Google data is never used for ads or AI training.

If the app from the DMG won't open
  1. Open the DMG and drag Daling into Applications.
  2. Open Daling once; when the warning appears, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway and enter your password.
  4. If it says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Daling.app in Terminal and open it again.

How to run it
  1. Make it executable: chmod +x daling-*.AppImage (or Properties → "Allow executing file as program")
  2. Double-click it, or run ./daling-*.AppImage
  3. If system libraries for the LMS sign-in browser are missing, the app shows the command to install them.
  4. Korean input works with ibus.
  5. Prefer an install? Get the .tar.gz, extract it and run ./install.sh.

Install without the warning

Paste into PowerShell on Windows, or Terminal on Mac and Linux. It checks the download against the GitHub release (SHA-256) before installing; run it again to update.

FAQ

Questions

Is this an official DGIST app?

No. It's an unofficial app made by a DGIST student and is not made or operated by DGIST. Always double-check important deadlines and notices on the LMS and official channels.

Does uninstalling delete my files?

No. Files and settings stay in the data folder (Windows C:\lms-autosaver, Mac and Linux ~/.lms-autosaver). Delete that folder if you no longer need it.

Where does the AI assistant send my mail?

The AI assistant (translation, plain-language search, mail drafts) is an optional Labs feature that's off until you turn it on in Settings. Even then, only when you press one of its buttons does it send the needed text directly to the AI you installed and signed in to on your PC (Claude, Gemini or Codex). It never goes through the developer, and nothing from Google Drive or Calendar is sent. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where do I send feedback or bug reports?

Use the "Feedback" button at the bottom left of the app, or write to tutleblue12@gmail.com.
